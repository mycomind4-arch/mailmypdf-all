# MailMyPDF Small Business Site Design

## Position

MailMyPDF Small Business is the operational correspondence and physical-mail workspace for small businesses.

Primary message: **Prepare business documents. Mail them professionally. Keep the record.**

## Visual direction

Professional, efficient, and operational. Use the MailMyPDF family document aesthetic while allowing a more business-oriented tone than Appeal Mail or Notice Respond.

## Homepage

1. Hero focused on eliminating printing, envelopes, stamps, and post-office work.
2. Workflow strip: Prepare → Review → Address → Mail → Track → Reuse.
3. Common business correspondence directory.
4. Reusable recipient/template workflow.
5. Mailing history and proof visualization.
6. Team/business workflow benefits where actually implemented.
7. Trust/security.
8. MailMyPDF fulfillment explanation.
9. FAQ.
10. Ecosystem footer.

## Application shell

Document → Recipient → Options → Review → Mail → Tracking → History

Make recipient, mailing method, document status, and fulfillment state immediately visible.

## Product feel

Prioritize speed and clarity over dense analytics. The user should understand the cost, mailing method, recipient, and final document before authorization.

## Guardrails

Do not advertise team, bulk, integrations, or automation capabilities unless they are actually implemented. Never show a successful mailing until the real fulfillment provider confirms it.
