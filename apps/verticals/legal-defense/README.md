# Legal Defense

Legal Defense is the MailMyPDF vertical for evidence-first criminal-defense preparation. It does not act as counsel or automatically file legal motions. It reconstructs events, preserves provenance, exposes contradictions, maps downstream evidence, and produces a reviewable packet for a defendant and licensed attorney.

## First executable workflow

`wrongful-stolen-vehicle-arrest` covers the chain:

`traffic stop → alleged stolen-vehicle basis → arrest → search → alleged paraphernalia`

The workflow produces:

- a Stop–Arrest–Search Timeline;
- a Vehicle Provenance Graph;
- a police-basis and identifier comparison;
- an Evidence Dependency Tree;
- a preservation and discovery matrix;
- a seller-scenario assessment; and
- an attorney-ready Defense Intelligence Packet.

California is the first reviewed authority pack. The runtime must warn when another jurisdiction is selected and must never invent deadlines, motions, or legal conclusions.

## Boundaries

- Rich execution requires the shared MailMyPDF account.
- No vertical-local authentication, payment, or mailing implementation.
- No automatic filing, seller contact, witness contact, or law-enforcement contact.
- No suppression guarantee. The output identifies facts and issues for counsel review.
- All consequential outputs require human review.
