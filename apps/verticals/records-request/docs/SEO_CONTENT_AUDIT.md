## Conversion

Primary CTA: **Tell us what records you need**.

The user starts in plain English. The system turns the objective into request categories, date ranges, custodians, identifiers, formats, exclusions, and jurisdiction-specific language.

The product's strongest differentiator is the second half: **request → production → audit**. Marketing should make that explicit.

## Content safeguards

Do not state that a government agency is violating a records law solely because a requested record was not produced. Use evidence-backed language such as "not located in the production" until the record-search facts support a stronger conclusion.
