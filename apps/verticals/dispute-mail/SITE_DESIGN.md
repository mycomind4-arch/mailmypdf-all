# Dispute Mail Site Design

## Position

Dispute Mail is the consumer dispute correspondence product in the MailMyPDF family.

Primary message: **Dispute the error. Back it with evidence. Keep the proof.**

## Visual direction

Keep the current teal/rose identity, but bring the overall hierarchy and restraint closer to Notice Respond. The site should feel confident and practical rather than aggressive or sales-heavy.

Tone: assertive, practical, protective.

## Homepage

1. Hero focused on identifying the error.
2. Visible workflow: Identify → Evidence → Draft → Review → Mail → Track → Prove.
3. Dispute-type directory: credit report, debt validation, billing, unauthorized charge, and additional verified workflows.
4. Deadline awareness and evidence requirements.
5. Guided analysis/drafting capabilities.
6. Concrete dispute case visualization.
7. Trust and privacy section.
8. MailMyPDF bridge for certified mail and proof.
9. FAQ.
10. Ecosystem footer.

## Workflow pages

Each dispute workflow should explain:

- exact problem
- recipient
- useful documents
- deadline considerations when supported by authoritative source material
- what the system analyzes
- evidence needed
- resulting correspondence
- review requirements
- mailing/receipt process

## Application shell

Use:

Issue → Documents → Analysis → Evidence → Draft → Review → Mail → Proof

Make deadlines and evidence gaps visible. Do not generate unsupported legal claims.

## Trust

Emphasize user control, source-grounded drafting, no fabricated facts, explicit mailing authorization, and proof of submission.

## MailMyPDF

The final step should be an obvious transition from approved dispute letter to physical mailing. Preserve the existing fulfillment integration and do not fake a successful mailing.

## Guardrails

No legal-advice claims. No fabricated deadlines or outcomes. Catalog entries may exist for discovery, but only genuinely implemented capabilities enter executable runtime.
