# Dispute Mail — SEO Content Audit

## Verdict
**Keep the guided dispute engine but reorganize acquisition around specific dispute jobs.**

The current homepage has strong workflow positioning for credit-report errors, debt validation, billing errors, and unauthorized charges, plus deadline awareness and documented mailing. fileciteturn34file0 The main weakness is that the broad "dispute" term is ambiguous; SEO needs situation-specific landing pages.

## Primary search intent

- dispute a credit report error
- dispute inaccurate information on credit report
- debt validation letter
- dispute a medical bill
- dispute a billing error
- dispute an unauthorized charge in writing
- write a dispute letter
- send a dispute letter certified mail

## Content architecture

### Commercial workflow pages
- `/dispute-a-credit-report-error`
- `/send-a-debt-validation-letter`
- `/dispute-a-medical-bill`
- `/dispute-a-billing-error`
- `/dispute-an-unauthorized-charge`
- `/write-a-dispute-letter`
- `/send-a-dispute-letter`

### Education
- dispute letter checklist
- evidence to include in a credit dispute
- debt validation request checklist
- how to document a billing dispute
- certified mail for disputes

## Conversion

Primary CTA should change by intent: **Start a credit dispute**, **Prepare a debt validation request**, etc. The product should collect the underlying evidence before drafting and keep every material claim traceable to user-provided information.

## Legal/content safeguards

Avoid universal statements about statutory deadlines. The current homepage contains deadline claims; these should be converted into source-backed, jurisdiction/context-aware guidance. fileciteturn34file0
