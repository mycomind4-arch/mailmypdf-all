## Workflow SEO rules

Each workflow should get:

1. One canonical landing page targeting the primary keyword.
2. A semantically complete supporting cluster using the related keywords above.
3. Exact workflow-specific intake language matching search intent.
4. Internal links from the parent insurance-claims topic hub.
5. FAQ/schema content around the real workflow questions represented in the keyword cluster.
6. No unsupported promises of coverage, entitlement, legal outcomes, or claim success.

## Build priority

Tier 1: denied claim, auto claim, roof damage, water damage, new claim.

Tier 2: claim dispute, coverage denial, property damage, fire/smoke, medical denial.

Tier 3: underpaid claim, commercial property, life insurance denial, disability denial, theft/vandalism.

The highest commercial-intent opportunities are not necessarily the largest-volume keywords. `car insurance claim denied` and `insurance claim denied` are examples of lower-volume but very high-CPC problem-aware searches.
