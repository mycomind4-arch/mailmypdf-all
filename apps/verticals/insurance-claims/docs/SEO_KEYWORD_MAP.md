# Insurance Claims SEO Keyword Map

Research basis: Keyword.com US English keyword discovery, August 2026.

## Strategy

Insurance Claims should own **claim denial / coverage dispute / appeal / underpayment** intent. The highest-value terms are highly commercial and often have high CPC.

## Priority clusters

### Core claim denial
- insurance claim denied — 1,300/mo
- denied claim — 1,900/mo
- insurance company denied claim — 140/mo
- denied insurance claim — 110/mo
- my insurance company denied my claim — 40/mo

### Appeals / disputes
- appealing insurance denial — 260/mo
- appealing a claim — 260/mo
- dispute insurance claim — 210/mo
- appealing an insurance claim — 210/mo
- coverage denial — 170/mo
- denied coverage — 170/mo

### High-value claim types
- roof insurance claim denied — 320/mo
- home insurance claim denied — 110/mo
- disability insurance denied — 110/mo
- life insurance claim denied — 210/mo
- workers comp denied claim — 210/mo
- auto insurance claim denied — 90/mo
- medical insurance claim denial — 320/mo

## Recommended architecture

Insurance Claims
├── Claim denied
├── Coverage denied
├── Underpaid claim
├── Claim documentation
├── Appeal an insurance denial
├── Home / roof claims
├── Health / medical claims
├── Disability claims
├── Workers compensation
├── Auto claims
└── Life insurance claims

## Rule

Do not compete with lawyers by promising legal outcomes. Sell the evidence/organization/response workflow: claim record, documents, chronology, gaps, response/appeal draft, review, and proof of submission.
