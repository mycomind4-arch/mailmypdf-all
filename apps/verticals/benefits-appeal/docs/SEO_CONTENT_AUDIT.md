# Benefits Appeal — SEO Content Audit

## Verdict
**Keep the product and analysis engine. Rewrite/expand the acquisition content around high-intent appeal searches.**

The current homepage is feature-rich and technically differentiated: Appeal X-Ray, timeline reconstruction, stress testing, evidence gaps, deadline analysis, and proof of timely filing. fileciteturn33file0 The weakness is that the hero starts with the product concept rather than the searcher's situation, and the page relies heavily on feature language.

## Primary search intent

People are usually searching one of these jobs:
- how to appeal a denied claim
- how to appeal a government decision
- how to appeal a benefits decision
- how to write an appeal letter
- appeal letter template
- request reconsideration
- send an appeal letter certified mail
- appeal a denial before the deadline

## New content architecture

### Commercial / workflow pages
- `/appeal-a-denied-claim`
- `/appeal-a-government-decision`
- `/appeal-a-benefits-decision`
- `/write-an-appeal-letter`
- `/request-reconsideration`
- `/send-an-appeal-letter`
- `/proof-of-timely-filing`

### Educational support
- how to write an appeal letter
- what to include in an appeal letter
- appeal deadline checklist
- reconsideration vs appeal
- how to organize evidence for an appeal
- certified mail for appeal documents

## Conversion strategy

Primary CTA: **Upload the decision letter**.

The visitor should move through: Decision → Deadline → Evidence → Appeal grounds → Draft → Stress test → Send → Proof.

## Content rules

Do not promise that an appeal will succeed. Never state a deadline unless sourced to the relevant authority and jurisdiction. Keep legal disclaimers contextual rather than allowing them to dominate the page.

## Priority

P0: rewrite homepage title/description/hero around "appeal a decision" and add workflow pages.
P1: strengthen internal linking from resources to workflows.
P2: build jurisdiction/decision-type pages only where genuinely useful, not thin keyword permutations.
