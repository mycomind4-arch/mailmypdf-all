# Private Office — Ecosystem SEO Standard

Private Office is the premium matter-centric layer of the MailMyPDF ecosystem.

## Public hierarchy
- `/` — premium pillar page: consequential matters, evidence, controlled AI assistance, human approval, mailing and proof.
- `/workflows` — searchable master workflow library.
- `/workflows/:id` — authority page for each matter type.
- `/how-it-works`, `/products`, `/pricing`, `/mail-a-pdf` — supporting product pages.

## UX model
The visible experience remains simple: define the matter, provide the record, review the system's findings, approve the exact correspondence, then send and preserve proof.

The underlying system may use multi-LLM advisory analysis, evidence reconciliation, provenance, validation, persistence, and fulfillment gates, but those mechanics should not become unnecessary user-facing steps.

## Visual model
Private Office remains the darkest, most serious and most premium layer in the ecosystem. Use restrained ivory, charcoal, navy and brass, editorial typography, substantial imagery, precise spacing, and command-center-grade authenticated workflows.

## Navigation lock
Do not add vertical-specific navigation items to the ecosystem shell.
