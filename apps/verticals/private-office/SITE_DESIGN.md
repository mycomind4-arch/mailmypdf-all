# Private Office Site Design

## Position

Private Office is the premium correspondence and evidence documentation product in the MailMyPDF family.

Primary message: **High-stakes correspondence, professionally prepared, provably delivered, and permanently documented.**

## Visual direction

Indigo and gold identity — serious, professional, premium. Not aggressive or sales-heavy. The site should feel like a private office: quiet, capable, and trustworthy.

Tone: professional, precise, protective.

## Homepage

1. Hero focused on the lifecycle: Prepare → Review → Approve → Deliver → Prove.
2. Matter-centric workflow positioning.
3. Evidence-grounded drafting with provenance.
4. Approval-gated delivery.
5. Certified mail with proof of delivery.
6. Privacy and security.
7. FAQ.
8. Ecosystem footer.

## Workflow pages

Each workflow should explain:
- exact problem
- recipient
- useful documents
- deadline considerations
- what the system analyzes
- evidence needed
- resulting correspondence
- review requirements
- mailing/receipt process

## Application shell

Matter lifecycle:
Intake → Documents → Facts → Evidence → Timeline → Analysis → Findings → Draft → Review → Approval → Fulfillment → Delivery/Proof → Follow-up → Escalation

Make deadlines, evidence gaps, and blocking issues visible. Do not generate unsupported legal claims.

## Trust

Emphasize user control, source-grounded drafting, no fabricated facts, explicit mailing authorization, and proof of submission.

## Guardrails

No legal-advice claims. No fabricated deadlines or outcomes. Consequential actions are approval-gated and server-side authorized.
