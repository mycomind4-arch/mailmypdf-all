# Notice Respond — SEO Content Audit

## Verdict
**Keep the guided notice workflow, but reposition the acquisition content around the user's received document and next action.**

The current homepage already targets government notices and offers guided workflows, AI drafting, tracking, certified mail, and proof of delivery. fileciteturn32file0 The main opportunity is to move from broad feature claims to high-intent situation pages.

## Primary search intent

- what to do after receiving a government notice
- how to respond to a government notice
- how to respond to an IRS notice
- how to respond to a compliance notice
- how to respond to a code enforcement notice
- how to respond to an agency decision
- government notice response letter
- notice response letter template

## Content architecture

### Core workflow pages
- `/respond-to-a-government-notice`
- `/respond-to-an-irs-notice`
- `/respond-to-a-code-enforcement-notice`
- `/respond-to-a-compliance-notice`
- `/respond-to-an-agency-decision`
- `/write-a-notice-response`
- `/send-a-notice-response`

### Education
- how to read a government notice
- notice response checklist
- how to find the deadline in a notice
- what documents to gather before responding
- how to preserve proof of a notice response

## Conversion

Primary CTA: **Upload your notice**.

The product should immediately extract sender, reference number, dates, requested action, deadlines, cited documents, and unresolved questions. The user then reviews the analysis before drafting.

## Content safeguards

Never imply the product determines the legal meaning of a notice or guarantees that a response satisfies a legal requirement. Jurisdiction-specific deadline claims must have a source and effective date.
