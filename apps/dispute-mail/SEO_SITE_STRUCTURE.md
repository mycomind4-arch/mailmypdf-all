# Dispute Mail — Canonical SEO Site Structure

Preserve the ecosystem-wide navigation exactly. This file defines SEO/content hierarchy and does not remove or rename working routes.

## Public
- `/` — Dispute Mail pillar: dispute inaccurate charges, accounts, records and claims; dispute types; how it works; what to gather; AI handles heavy lifting; review; mailing/proof; featured workflows; FAQ; start.
- `/workflows` — Dispute Workflows hub with situation discovery, search, category navigation and crawlable authority pages.
- `/how-it-works` — upload documents → AI understands dispute → organize facts/evidence → build dispute → review/approve → MailMyPDF sends → track/preserve proof.
- `/products`, `/pricing`, `/mail-a-pdf` — ecosystem/product pages.
- `/about`, `/contact`, `/faq`, `/resources/*` — supporting SEO/footer content.
- `/privacy`, `/terms`, `/404` — legal/error.

## Workflow taxonomy
Debt & collections; credit reporting; billing & payments; loans & financial accounts; contracts & services; follow-up & escalation; federal consumer protection.

## SEO flow
Pillar → workflow hub → specific dispute authority page → authenticated workflow. Preserve existing `/dispute/*` and `/workflows/*` URLs; never remove a working URL just to simplify the taxonomy.

## Application routes
Auth, dashboard, account, start/analyze and authenticated workflow routes remain functional but outside primary SEO navigation.

## Simplicity rule
AI performs document analysis, dispute classification, fact/timeline extraction, discrepancy identification, evidence organization, authority research when required, risk/strategy assistance, drafting, validation and recipient checks. The user reviews and approves.

## Navigation lock
No vertical-specific navigation additions. Ecosystem shell remains authoritative.
