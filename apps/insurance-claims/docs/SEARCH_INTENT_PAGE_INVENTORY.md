# Insurance Claims — Search-Intent Page & Workflow Inventory

## Architecture
Insurance Claims is the master vertical for understanding, documenting, disputing, appealing, and tracking insurance claims. It should be organized by claim type and failure mode, with strong evidence/document workflows.

## Tier 3 — Workflow pages
### Claim intake and review
- Upload claim/denial documents
- Build claim timeline
- Identify coverage position
- Extract adjuster/requested evidence
- Organize supporting evidence

### Denied claims
- Analyze denial reasons
- Challenge factual errors
- Request reconsideration
- Prepare internal appeal
- Escalate an unresolved claim

### Specialized claims
- Health/medical claim denial
- Home/roof/water damage claim denial
- Auto claim denial
- Workers’ compensation denial
- Short/long-term disability denial
- Life insurance denial

## SEO rules
Avoid creating pages for every insurer name unless the workflow, policy language, requirements, or submission path materially differs. Use insurer-specific information as supporting content under the relevant claim-type page when appropriate.

## Conversion path
Search → claim type → upload denial/evidence → analyze → build appeal/dispute → review → mail/proof → response tracking.
