# MailMyPDF Ecosystem Workflow Architecture

MailMyPDF is the transaction and fulfillment layer for a family of focused workflow products.

## Public discovery
Each vertical owns a clear pillar page, workflow directory, and intent-specific authority pages. Search traffic should land directly on the most relevant workflow without requiring users to understand the ecosystem first.

## Shared user model
1. Describe or upload the document/problem.
2. Let AI perform the heavy analysis underneath.
3. Review the facts, evidence, draft and mailing details.
4. Explicitly approve the exact output.
5. Mail through MailMyPDF.
6. Preserve tracking and proof.

## Navigation lock
The ecosystem navigation stays shared and minimal. Vertical SEO architecture should expand through pages and workflow discovery, not through additional top-level navigation items.

## Visual progression
MailMyPDF stays approachable and document-focused. Vertical landing pages are lighter and search-friendly. Workflow authority pages become more serious and evidence-oriented. Private Office remains the deepest command-center experience.
