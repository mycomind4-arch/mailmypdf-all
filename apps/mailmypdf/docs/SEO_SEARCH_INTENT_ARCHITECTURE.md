# MailMyPDF Search-Intent SEO Architecture

## Positioning

MailMyPDF should optimize around the user's job, not the underlying mechanism:

> Send important documents. Keep the proof.

The core journey is **Prepare → Send → Track → Prove**. Situation-specific verticals expand the acquisition surface: respond to a notice, dispute something, appeal a decision, request records, or communicate with a government agency.

## Intent hierarchy

### 1. Transactional

Primary landing pages:

- `/send-a-letter-online`
- `/send-pdf-by-mail`
- `/certified-mail-online`
- `/registered-mail-online`
- `/proof-of-mailing`
- `/proof-of-delivery`

### 2. Problem-solving

- `/how-to-send-certified-mail`
- `/how-to-prove-you-mailed-a-letter`
- `/how-to-prove-someone-received-a-letter`
- `/how-to-send-an-important-document`

### 3. Situation-specific

Build high-quality pages around real workflows rather than thin keyword variants:

- responding to government notices
- legal notices
- disputes
- appeals
- public-records requests
- code-enforcement correspondence
- agency correspondence

### 4. AI/document intent

Target users who already possess a document but do not know what to do next. The primary CTA should be **Upload your document**, not a generic signup.

## Conversion architecture

Search page → situation/document intake → analysis → draft/prepare → mailing method → proof record → follow-up.

Every SEO landing page should connect the user's search problem to an immediate product action. Avoid informational pages that terminate at an unrelated signup.

## Homepage message

Recommended hero:

**Send Important Documents. Keep the Proof.**

Upload a document, address it, choose how you want it mailed, and keep a complete record of what you sent and what happened afterward.

Primary CTA: **Upload a Document**

Secondary pathways:
- I received a notice
- I need to send certified mail
- I need proof of delivery
- I need to dispute something
- I need to appeal a decision
- I need to request records

## SEO rules

1. Optimize for intent clusters, not isolated keywords.
2. Prefer one excellent page for a meaningful intent over many thin variants.
3. Situation pages must contain useful workflow guidance and a relevant product action.
4. Jurisdiction pages must contain real jurisdiction-specific information; never mass-produce placeholder substitutions.
5. Internal links should connect Problem → Solution → Product → Delivery → Proof.
6. Track organic conversion to completed documented mailing, not traffic alone.
7. Do not make unsupported legal claims. Explain procedures and clearly identify uncertainty.

## Measurement

Track:

- organic impressions
- rankings by intent cluster
- organic CTR
- document uploads
- notice uploads
- response generation
- certified-mail selection
- completed mailings
- repeat mailings
- revenue per organic visitor
- revenue per landing page

## Ecosystem relationship

MailMyPDF is the delivery/proof layer for the vertical ecosystem. Vertical products capture situation-specific intent, while MailMyPDF provides the common Prepare → Send → Track → Prove workflow.
