# Appeal a Denied Claim — Gold Standard

## Purpose

Turn a denied insurance claim or other adverse claim decision into a traceable, review-ready response package without inventing facts or presenting uncertain rules as universal.

## Customer promise

MailMyPDF organizes the source decision, stated denial reason, dates, evidence, response grounds, draft, approval, mailing record, tracking, and proof in one workflow.

## Required source material

- Denial or adverse decision notice
- Claim/reference number
- Policy, plan, or agreement documents when available
- Relevant correspondence
- Supporting records directly tied to the disputed claim
- Any appeal or response instructions contained in the notice

## Deterministic checks

The workflow must preserve source provenance for extracted decision facts, reference identifiers, dates, deadlines, denial reasons, and cited requirements. Unknown values remain unknown rather than being inferred.

The draft must not assert coverage, eligibility, medical facts, damages, policy language, or deadlines that are not supported by the available record or verified authority.

The final stress test must detect unresolved contradictions between the denial and the supporting record, missing evidence for material assertions, inconsistent dates or reference numbers, missing recipient instructions, and unsupported requested relief.

## Health-insurance authority notes

The NAIC explains that a denied health-insurance claim can involve an internal appeal and, where applicable, external review, and that the controlling deadlines depend on the applicable plan and rules. ## Escalation

When an insurer continues to deny a claim, the workflow should surface the applicable state insurance regulator as a possible escalation path. The NAIC states that state insurance departments can investigate complaints involving unfair claim delays or denials and other insurance issues. citeturn957525search1turn957525search3

## Review gate

No draft, approval, or payment event is treated as mailing success. The locked Benefits Appeal path is document → analysis → draft → validation → human review → approval → payment → MailMyPDF → tracking → proof.
