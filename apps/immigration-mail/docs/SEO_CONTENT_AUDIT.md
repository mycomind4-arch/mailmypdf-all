# Immigration Mail — SEO Content Audit

## Verdict
**Keep the workflow engine, but make the content intent-specific and document-first.**

The existing homepage is positioned as immigration correspondence and already emphasizes guided workflows, AI drafting, USPS delivery, and proof. fileciteturn35file0 The missing layer is strong search coverage for the specific document or notice the visitor has in hand.

## Primary search intent

- how to respond to an immigration notice
- immigration response letter
- USCIS response letter
- respond to a USCIS notice
- request USCIS records
- immigration cover letter
- immigration evidence cover letter
- send immigration documents by mail
- proof of mailing immigration documents

## Content architecture

### Workflow pages
- `/respond-to-a-uscis-notice`
- `/respond-to-an-immigration-notice`
- `/write-an-immigration-cover-letter`
- `/prepare-an-immigration-response`
- `/send-immigration-documents-by-mail`
- `/proof-of-mailing-immigration-documents`

### Education
- how to organize documents for an immigration response
- immigration response checklist
- cover letter vs response letter
- preserving a mailing record for important immigration correspondence

## Conversion

Primary CTA: **Upload your immigration notice or document**.

Analyze document → identify requested items/deadlines → organize evidence → draft → review → send → preserve proof.

## Safeguards

Do not imply eligibility, approval likelihood, or legal advice. USCIS-specific procedural claims should link to authoritative sources and display their effective date where relevant.
