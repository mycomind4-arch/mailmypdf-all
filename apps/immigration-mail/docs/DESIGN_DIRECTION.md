# Immigration Mail — Product Design Direction

## Role in the MailMyPDF family

Immigration Mail is the document-and-correspondence preparation specialist for important immigration submissions and responses. It must feel careful, procedural, and document-centric.

Primary journey:

**Understand Request → Organize Documents → Identify Gaps → Prepare Correspondence → Review → Mail → Track → Prove**

## Design benchmark

Notice Respond establishes the family visual quality bar. Preserve Immigration Mail's existing warm postal/document language while making hierarchy, workflow discovery, mobile behavior, and review states equally polished.

## Product personality

Careful, calm, precise, organized. Avoid fear-based copy and avoid implying legal representation or guaranteed immigration outcomes.

## Visual language

- Warm paper and ink palette
- Postal red/stamp accents
- Serif editorial headings
- Monospace case/reference metadata
- Envelope, document, checklist, timeline, and packet motifs
- Generous whitespace and clear document hierarchy

## Homepage hierarchy

1. Prepare important immigration correspondence without a printer
2. Visual packet workflow
3. Guided workflow directory
4. Document organization/intelligence
5. Review and user-control safeguards
6. MailMyPDF fulfillment, tracking, and proof
7. FAQ and CTA

## Workflow pages

Each workflow page should explain the specific correspondence type, useful documents, information to review, packet organization, draft contents, review requirements, and mailing path. Avoid implying unsupported agency-specific automation.

## Workspace

Use a packet-oriented workspace:

**Request/notice → Required items → Documents → Facts → Draft → Packet review → Mailing → Proof**

Clearly distinguish user-provided facts from inferred or missing information.

## Safety

Never invent immigration facts, deadlines, forms, filing addresses, eligibility, legal conclusions, or agency outcomes. Where information is not verified, show an explicit review state.

## Definition of done

Immigration Mail should feel like a serious document-preparation product whose final action naturally becomes MailMyPDF fulfillment, without pretending to be an immigration law firm.
