# SSDI Denial Appeal — Workflow Standard

Updated: 2026-09-16

## Purpose

Turn an actual SSDI denial/decision into a source-grounded reconsideration/appeal packet without inventing medical facts, procedural rules, deadlines, recipients, or outcomes.

## Source-first requirements

The workflow should begin from the actual SSA notice and preserve the original source document.

Official SSA forms currently retained in this workflow tree:

- `forms/generated/ssa-561-u2.pdf`
- `forms/generated/ssa-3441.pdf`
- `forms/generated/ssa-827.pdf`

These source PDFs must not be casually recreated or replaced by lookalike documents. Where the workflow needs generated supplemental pages, keep those clearly separate from official forms.

## Safety boundaries

- Never fabricate diagnoses, treatment, symptoms, functional limitations, work history, or medical evidence.
- Never infer an appeal deadline solely from the notice date.
- Never assume the correct appeal level or filing method without support from the notice/current authoritative SSA information.
- Never invent an SSA office, recipient, form requirement, or delivery address.
- Keep extracted facts, user-supplied facts, and procedural conclusions distinct.
- Require explicit human review before final approval and any mailing action.

## Expected workflow capabilities

The SSDI workflow should support:

1. denial/decision upload and analysis;
2. claimant/intake facts;
3. medical and work-history information;
4. supporting evidence upload and retention;
5. mapping confirmed information into required official forms;
6. validation/readiness checks;
7. generated cover/supplemental material where needed;
8. final packet assembly;
9. exact-document review and approval;
10. payment and mailing fulfillment;
11. tracking/proof retention.

## Pricing

Do not carry forward retired hard-coded launch prices from the legacy documentation. Use the current shared pricing system and calculate the final price from the approved packet and mailing method.
