# @mailmypdf/identity-capacity

Shared deterministic engines for identity and capacity reasoning.

Current foundation:
- name normalization and comparison
- purpose-specific source authority evaluation
- authoritative-name resolution
- explicit conflict and human-review gates
- provenance-preserving intelligence findings

These engines compare and resolve evidence for specific purposes. They do not treat typography, capitalization, aliases, or a filing by itself as proof that two records identify the same legal person or entity.
