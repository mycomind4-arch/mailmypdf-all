# MailMyPDF Brand + Search Intent Rebuild

## Canonical customer-facing story

**MailMyPDF — Prepare. Send. Prove.**

MailMyPDF is the delivery and document-workflow platform. Customers may arrive because they need to send a PDF, respond to a notice, appeal a decision, dispute something, or request records. They should not need to understand an internal ecosystem or product umbrella before getting help.

## Brand hierarchy

- **MailMyPDF** is the customer-facing master brand.
- Solution names describe situations and workflows, not an umbrella brand.
- Repository names remain implementation boundaries and do not need to appear as consumer-facing brands.
- BureaucracyOS is retired from customer-facing positioning.

## Search ownership

### MailMyPDF owns transactional delivery intent

Examples:
- send a PDF by mail
- mail a letter online
- send certified mail online
- print and mail a document
- proof of mailing
- proof of delivery

### Solution verticals own problem/situation intent

Examples:
- respond to a government notice
- respond to a code enforcement notice
- appeal a decision
- dispute a debt or charge
- request public records
- respond to a landlord notice
- organize an insurance claim

Vertical pages should explain the problem first, demonstrate the workflow, and hand off to MailMyPDF for final document delivery.

## UX rule

Do not make users choose between a collection of product names. Ask what they are trying to accomplish.

Preferred navigation concepts:

- Send a document
- Respond to a notice
- Appeal a decision
- Dispute something
- Request records

## SEO rule

Do not create pages solely because a keyword can be made into a URL. Each page needs a distinct search intent, useful original content, a clear next action, and a relationship to the appropriate workflow.

## Trust rule

Privacy, document handling, mailing records, and pricing clarity belong near conversion points. Important-document users should not have to hunt for trust information in the footer.

## Pricing rule

Lead with the starting price only when the page also makes the final price calculation understandable before checkout. Mailing-class and page-count variables should be transparent rather than hidden behind the headline price.

## Product-preview rule

Do not use invented customer records as trust-building product evidence. Product mockups should be clearly illustrative or use safe, obviously synthetic examples that demonstrate the actual workflow.

## Legal-positioning rule

Describe document organization, analysis, drafting assistance, evidence organization, and delivery. Do not imply that MailMyPDF is a lawyer, guarantees legal outcomes, determines legal rights, or guarantees that a particular mailing method satisfies a jurisdiction-specific service requirement.
